How these rankings work

Each team plays each of the other 31 once on a neutral field. Its score is the average win chance. Home field is fit from nflverse and is not added here, so a venue cannot move a rank.

EPA margin is the schedule-adjusted rating underneath that chance. Schedule is the average final EPA of opponents already played. Wins above expected uses frozen pregame chances, not this season-long rating.
